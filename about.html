About the garage

The International Porsche Garage is a specialist publisher of digital Porsche workshop and service manuals. We built the library we always wanted as Porsche owners: complete, clearly organized, and available the same day — no waiting for a binder in the mail.

Every manual is delivered electronically. You buy on eBay, we send your secure access instructions through eBay messages, and you're reading the same day — on any phone, tablet, or computer.

Is anything physically shipped?
No. All products are digital downloads. Nothing is shipped — ever. Any listing mentioning physical media is a legacy description; delivery is always electronic via eBay messages.
How fast is delivery?
Access instructions are sent through your eBay messages shortly after your payment clears — usually within hours.
What format are the manuals?
Digital documents readable on any modern phone, tablet, or computer — no special software required.

Search the store for your model — coverage spans air-cooled classics through current models. If you can't find it, ask us.
